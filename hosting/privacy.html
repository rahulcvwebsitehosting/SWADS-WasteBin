Privacy Policy

This policy explains what SWADS collects, why it is used, and the choices available to users.

Information we process

  • Google account identity information supplied during sign-in, such as email address and Firebase user identifier.
  • A Google OAuth refresh token for the gmail.send permission, stored by the SWADS backend so authorised clearance emails can be sent.
  • Notification tokens used to deliver app alerts.
  • Registered-bin data, device identifiers, fill measurements, alert history, and location information entered for a bin, including address, PIN code, or coordinates.

How information is used

We use this information to authenticate users, associate bins with their owners, show live status, process fill and reset events, deliver notifications, and send waste-clearance requests from the consenting user's Gmail account. SWADS does not read, modify, or delete Gmail messages.

Google user data

SWADS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the user-facing features described here and is not sold, used for advertising, or transferred for unrelated purposes.

Storage, protection, and retention

Application data is stored in Firebase services with access controls. OAuth refresh tokens are kept in backend-only records and are not readable by the Android client. Data is retained while needed to operate the service, meet security obligations, or until deletion is requested.

Your choices

You can revoke SWADS access at any time from your Google Account's third-party access settings. You may also request access to or deletion of your SWADS account data by emailing rahulshyamcv@gmail.com. Revoking Google access prevents future Gmail sending but does not automatically delete operational records already stored by SWADS.

Sharing

SWADS shares only the information necessary to operate its service providers and send clearance requests. Clearance messages may include the registered bin's location and identifiers and are delivered to relevant municipal or recycling contacts. We do not sell personal information.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. For privacy questions or requests, contact rahulshyamcv@gmail.com.
